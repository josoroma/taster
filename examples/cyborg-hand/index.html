DEXTRA / CONCEPT NOTES

A machine with
a human gesture.

DEXTRA is an original robotic-hand concept. Its connected finger links rotate around defined joint pivots; the controls and angle readouts use the same kinematic model.

The cutaway separates the outer shell and internal assemblies along authored inspection paths. It explains the arrangement of parts, rather than documenting a manufacturing or servicing procedure.

Joint limits and closure timing belong to this design. It does not calculate motor torque, contact forces, collision avoidance, or safe grip pressure. The meshes are illustrative geometry, not production CAD or a validated prosthesis.

All geometry and motion are rendered from code. Local assets keep the experience independent of remote rendering services.

THE EVIDENCE BEHIND THE VIEW

Coordinates, with context.

Crambin is shown from PDB entry 1CRN: chain A, 46 residues, 327 non-hydrogen atoms, X-ray diffraction, 1.50 Å resolution. The local PDB file retains the deposited coordinates in ångströms.

Helix and strand assignments and the three disulfide pairs come from the file’s HELIX, SHEET and SSBOND records. Sulfur distances are calculated directly from SG atom coordinates. Helix termini include the distortions described in the source records.

The ribbon interpolates alpha-carbon positions. Width, atom radii and colors serve readability. Ordinary sticks use a local distance rule within residues and between adjacent peptide groups; bond orders are not assigned. No hydrogens, solvent, electron-density map or molecular surface are added.

Playback changes the representation and follows the chain. It does not move atoms, simulate folding, reproduce a trajectory or predict binding. This is a small, static experimental model in a crystal context.

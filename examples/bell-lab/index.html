WHAT THIS EXPLAINS

Exact state.
Imagined machine.

Bell Lab computes an ideal, noiseless two-qubit pure state. Gates multiply complex amplitudes; probability is amplitude magnitude squared. Qubit ordering is |q₀ q₁⟩, with qubit 0 on the left.

The Bloch arrows show each qubit’s reduced state. They shrink to the center for a maximally entangled pair: neither local view alone describes the joint state. The ribbon and layered machine are explanatory metaphors, not photon paths or a real processor’s construction.

Measurement samples the joint computational-basis distribution and collapses this simulated state. Matching 00/11 counts alone do not certify entanglement; the full state and its relative phase matter. Entanglement does not permit faster-than-light communication.

The guided tour shows ideal gates at chapter boundaries. Decorative motion between them is illustrative; it is not a simulated physical gate pulse.

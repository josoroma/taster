THE EVIDENCE BEHIND THE VIEW

Wonder, with a map.

This is an educational journey through established particle physics. Shapes, colors, paths, and display rotation are visual devices. Transitions switch between scale regimes; they are not a single physically scaled flight.

Electron clouds illustrate probability, not classical orbits. Nucleon diagrams show valence identity within a more complex system of quarks, antiquarks, and gluons. Quantum spin is intrinsic angular momentum, not literal rotation. The final stop marks open questions, not confirmed smaller constituents.

About this little book

Original text, SVG artwork, and code by Taster. This is a three-chapter lesson, not a reproduction or extraction of a book.

The model

Two sinusoidal waves travel in the same direction with equal amplitude, frequency, and wavelength. We add signed displacements in an ideal linear medium. The plots share a scale of 24 drawing units per centimetre; motion runs at one quarter of model speed. No boundaries, damping, energy calculation, or nonlinear effects are simulated.

y₁ = A sin(kx − ωt)
y₂ = A sin(kx − ωt + φ)
Aₛᵤₘ = 2A |cos(φ/2)|

NOTES ON THIS MODEL

A map, not the territory.

The geometry, folds and lobe borders are a procedural educational schematic, not a clinical atlas or a scan. Colors identify regions; they are not the colors of living tissue. Size and visibility of deep structures are simplified.

The four highlighted cortical lobes occur in both hemispheres. The cerebrum is continuous tissue: pulling the regions apart is an inspection device, not a biological process. Other important structures are omitted.

Brain functions depend on networks. The roles shown here are introductory examples, not exclusive assignments. This model does not support “left-brained versus right-brained” personality claims.

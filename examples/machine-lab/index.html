How the motion is computed Equations, assumptions & sources +

Exact geometry. Prescribed speed.

Crank radius r = 40 mm; connecting rod l = 140 mm. The slider pin stays on the x axis. Angle θ is counterclockwise from +x. Distances are solved in metres; readouts convert to millimetres.

q = √(l² − r² sin²θ)
x = r cos θ + q
s = r + l − x
ds/dt = ω(r sin θ + r² sin θ cos θ / q)
ω = 2π · rpm / 60

What this study means

The display and numbers come from one closed-form kinematic model. Pausing freezes the angle; the velocity readout continues to show the derivative at the set speed. Playback runs in real time with no hidden speed factor.

This is an original ideal rigid-link schematic with frictionless joints. It does not solve force, torque, combustion, wear, collisions, or manufacturing tolerances. The lifted guard is an inspection aid.

Workflow inspiration: Airsup Machine Skill. Equation reference: its slider-crank solver recipe. Taster implementation and geometry are original; no upstream runtime is bundled.
